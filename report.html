Development process case study

Development report

A structured artifact designed to prove technical depth and professional judgment to more than one kind of reader.

The challenge of communication

The problem

The report needed to communicate technical and business competence to two reader types at once. That required a structure that could be read quickly but still support deeper scrutiny.

The structure behind the report

What I did

I used a thesis framework as the backbone and made the abstract and table of contents operate like self-routing mechanisms so the reader could move from overview to detail without losing context.

The value it created

What came of it

The report provided a deeper dive into the entire development process and showed how professionalism and execution were handled with a client-facing mindset rather than a purely technical one.
